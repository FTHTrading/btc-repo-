Protocol & Methodology

Complete transparent disclosures of data confidence levels, published emission factors, mathematical reward functions, and JSON schemas.

Real Measurement Hierarchy

Every recorded metric in the platform must display both what it measures and how trustworthy it is. Estimates are labeled and never treated as facts.

Data Status Meaning Example Source Evidence Multiplier
USER_ENTERED Manually supplied by the user "320 kWh entered from July utility statement" 0.2× (Provisional points)
RECEIPT_BACKED Supported by a receipt or invoice "Transit purchase receipt or electric bill PDF attached" 0.8× (High confidence)
METERED From a connected device, utility, or provider "Electricity usage imported from utility integration" 1.0× (Highest confidence)
ATTESTED Validated by an approved third party "Volunteer hours confirmed by partner organization" 1.0× (Highest confidence)
REGISTRY_VERIFIED Linked to an authoritative registry record "Carbon credit retirement certificate verified" 1.0× (Certificate validated)
ESTIMATED Derived from a disclosed factor/model "Travel estimate using route miles and vehicle mode" 0.3× (Provisional estimate)
UNVERIFIED Recorded for personal tracking; cannot earn rewards "Self-reported repair action without receipt" 0.0× (No reward)

Non-Biological Positive Activity Ledger

Human existence is treated as a sovereign source of positive value—not as a biological liability.

What We Measure & Reward
  • Focused deep work and skill acquisition
  • Caregiving and mentorship
  • Community and volunteer service
  • Physical product repair and reuse
  • Environmental stewardship
  • Metered energy and transit reductions
What We Never Charge or Penalize
  • Oxygen consumption or respiratory CO2
  • Biological metabolism or basal caloric needs
  • Bodily existence and sleep
  • Essential water and nourishment needs
  • Disability or medical device requirements
  • Personal health conditions

Versioned Reward Policy & Caps

Rules governing point accumulation and deterministic VTIME credit conversion.

VTIME issued = min( base_reward × evidence_multiplier × policy_multiplier, category_cap, daily_cap )
  • Published Reward Policy: Caps issuance per activity, per category (50 VTIME/day), and per account (200 VTIME/day).
  • Provisional Valuation: Self-reported entries receive a lower multiplier (0.2×) until receipt or meter confirmation is provided.
  • Anti-Duplication: Cryptographic SHA-256 hashes prevent duplicate receipt, session, or retirement claims.
  • Auditable Correction: If an entry is found invalid, points are revoked from the local and on-chain verification record.
  • No Offset Equivalence: One VTIME never equals one tonne of carbon. VTIME is an internal ecosystem utility credit.
