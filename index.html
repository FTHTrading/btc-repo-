Core Protocol Principles

Built exclusively on individual sovereignty, user-consented records, and non-extractive utility.

01. First-Person Agency

Participants alone start, commit, and finalize their focus sessions. No peer rankings, employer surveillance, manager ratings, or social scores are ever ingested.

02. Private by Default

Raw activity, journal notes, and biometric telemetry remain client-side encrypted. Only cryptographic hashes, ZK proofs, or minimal metadata are recorded.

03. Non-Transferable Utility

$VTIME operates strictly as a closed-loop internal accounting credit for voluntary session staking and tool access. No cash value, no secondary trading, no speculative markets.
