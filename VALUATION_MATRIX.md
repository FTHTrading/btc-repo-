## 1. Valuation Methodologies Applied

To provide an institutional-grade, realistic appraisal of the 304 repositories across `kevanbtc` and `unykornai`, three standard software & asset valuation models were synthesized:

1. **Cost-to-Replicate (Replacement Cost Method)**: Evaluates the total engineering hours, architectural design, security auditing, and test coverage required to recreate the codebases from scratch at market rates ($200/hr average senior Web3/Rust/C#/Python rate).
2. **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) of ARR (Monetization Potential)**: Calculates the net present value of recurring SaaS subscriptions, DEX fees, CBDC licensing, macro intelligence feeds, and AUM management fees.
3. **Underlying Asset Anchor & Collateral**: Direct linkage to verified physical assets, bond issuances ($5.0B Series B Note), $246M TVL in UnyKorn L1, and real-world asset ledgers ($4.82B UnyKorn AUC).
